# 1.2.0

- Draw on photos: the crop screen has a crop and a draw tab. Draw lines in any of the app's colours, at any thickness; the pencil remembers both.
- Rotate photos and videos a quarter turn from the crop screen.
- Hold an album, group or private album for a moment and drag it to start rearranging straight away, without waiting for the menu.
- While rearranging, drop an album onto a group to put it in, with undo. Works in Albums and in Favorites.
- Groups now always stay above albums.
- Moving a group above or below an open group works again.

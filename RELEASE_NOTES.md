# 1.5.1

- Opening or closing a group no longer bumps the albums under it twice: they follow the group down in one motion and stay where it ends.
- Share and PDF for selected photos now sit in their own bar above the main one, so the main bar keeps its room. PDF has a new icon.
- Renaming an album or private group has a colour wheel: pick any colour around the full circle and the name takes it. Reset puts the default back.

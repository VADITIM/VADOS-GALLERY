# v1.1.0

- Crop is in the photo's bar, between the heart and delete, and has its own violet.
- Opening crop moves the photo into place; closing moves it back.
- Pinch to zoom a photo under the crop frame.
- Reset, Revert and Save are at the bottom; the ratios sit in a nav-style pill.
- Trim ends have arrows that move them by a tenth of a second, and the preview follows the finger while dragging.
- New share icon, a larger lock icon.
- Week headers show only the calendar week.
- The photo count in the corner no longer jumps, and its numbers type themselves over.
